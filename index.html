Hi! I’m Sandra De Guzman, 27 years old and currently pursuing my degree. I started college a bit later than most due to personal matters, but I never gave up on my dream. I am the fourth in my family to go to college.

My goal in life is simple: to be happy. For me, happiness means finishing my studies and having my dream job. My dream is to become a successful businesswoman, earning enough to support my own needs and wants, and most importantly, to help my family.

I am currently taking a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Sorsogon State University – Bulan Campus.

Computer Science is not just about code, software, or technology. It’s about growing.

It’s about learning what I’m capable of, being brave enough to face challenges, and slowly building the future I want for myself. I’ve learned that success doesn’t happen overnight. It takes dedication, patience, and the courage to keep going, even when things get hard.

Being a student taught me that patience can open doors that knowledge alone can’t. Those experiences didn’t just make me a better student, they made me a better person. They taught me that real growth comes from both my wins and my mistakes.

I know I still have a lot to learn. I don’t have all the skills yet, and I don’t have all the answers. But I have the willingness to learn, the drive to work hard, and the openness to grow with every opportunity.

Just like the code I write, I’m still a work in progress, and I’m proud of every step that brings me closer to who I want to be.
